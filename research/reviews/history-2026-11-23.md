## For the environmental scientist
- Grazing retreat from the late 19th century is a human cause behind shrub and treeline rise. S50 (Swiss, abstract only) supports land abandonment as the main driver.
- Drailles climb straight up the slope. That links old animal paths to fall-line erosion (S31). It is an inference and needs your check.
