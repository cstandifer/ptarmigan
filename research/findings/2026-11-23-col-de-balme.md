# Col de Balme as the home ground (2026-11-23)

Researched because Caitlin leans toward Col de Balme for the view. Compared with Col du Bonhomme, where the best ptarmigan data is. Not yet reviewed by the avian expert.

## Evidence for ptarmigan at Col de Balme, by strength
| Evidence | Strength | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The col (2,191 m) sits inside the species' elevation band (2,000 to 3,000 m, snowbeds and moors) | Habitat fit only, not a sighting | S52, S54 |
| The Aiguilles Rouges reserves, across the Vallorcine side from Balme, list rock ptarmigan as an emblematic species | Nearby presence, not counts | S53 |
| Ptarmigan counts or sightings at Col de Balme itself | **None found** | |
| Reserve activity report 2025 may hold nearby counts | Not opened (download blocked) | S55 |
| Regional decline: Contamines reserve (Col du Bonhomme, about 40 km south-west) | Strong, but another site | S18 |
| Regional decline and ski-lift wire collisions, Italian Alps about 100 km east | Strong, regional | S46 |

## Human pressure at Col de Balme
| Trace | Source |
|---|---|
| TMB crosses the col | S52 |

The ski lifts link Balme to the one human threat measured in S46 (wire collisions), but that link is regional reasoning, not a Balme finding.

## Trade-off
| | Col de Balme | Col du Bonhomme |
|---|---|---|
| View of the massif and a glacier | Whole Mont Blanc chain, Glacier du Tour (S52) | Peaks and Tre-la-Tete side, less iconic |
| Local ptarmigan data | None found | Counts since 2008 (S18) |
| Local hiker data | None found | Eco-counter passages (S18) |
| Human traces | TMB, refuge, ski lifts | TMB, refuges, bivouac rules |

Suggested hybrid: set the scene at Col de Balme and show Bonhomme and Italian data with their place names, never relabeled as Balme.
